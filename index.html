Why it's built this way

Small on purpose.

EPKStay started with one property tucked into the California mountains — near a small town that serves as a gateway to Yosemite — and grew into a handful of ventures that share a philosophy more than a business model: do fewer things, do them ourselves, and keep the operation small enough that nothing gets outsourced to a stranger.

That's true of the stay — no answering service, no revolving door of cleaners we've never met — and it's true of the rest: a consulting practice built on real hiring experience, a dog-care routine run like clockwork, notes written from inside the work rather than about it, and a software product being built carefully rather than rushed.
